Set completion

Paste a Bindarr collection export to see how much of each set you own, by card and by printing, and get the list of what's missing.

1 · Paste your Bindarr export

Export your collection from Bindarr, filtered to a set or block or two, and paste the tab-separated text with its header row. Card data comes from Scryfall and is saved in this browser, so a set is downloaded at most once a day, and again only when Scryfall adds cards to it or after 7 days.

2 · Report

Build a report to see it here.

3 · Missing list

One line per missing card (By card, using its lowest-numbered printing) or per missing printing (By printing), as 1 Name (SET) number for a Moxfield or TCGplayer want list. The CSV adds rarity and version. It follows the List setting in the report.

Card data from Scryfall. Not affiliated with Scryfall, Bindarr, TCGplayer, ManaBox, Moxfield or Wizards of the Coast. Everything runs in your browser. Your export never leaves it: only set codes are sent to Scryfall's public API, and the card data that comes back is saved in this browser. Version dev · Releases
